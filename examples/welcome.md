# Welcome to your wiki

A small home for useful knowledge. Keep your notes in Markdown, share a link, and let your tools take care of the rest.

## Make knowledge easy to keep

Every page is a plain Markdown file. That means your content stays readable, portable, and easy to back up. There is no special document format to learn.

Use this space for things you want to find again:

- **Guides** that make a process easier to repeat.
- **Decisions** with the context behind them.
- **Notes** that turn a one-off discovery into shared knowledge.

> Start with one useful page. A good wiki grows from the work you actually do.

## Find your way around

Browse the pages in the sidebar, or search for a word you remember. Search looks through both titles and page content.

The outline on each article links to its sections. **View Markdown** opens the original source, ready to save or use in another tool.

## Write in a familiar format

Headings, links, lists, tables, and code blocks all have a place here. See the [Markdown guide](markdown-guide.md) for examples.

```markdown
# A useful idea

Write down the context, then the details.

## The next step

Make it easy for someone else to continue.
```

## Connect your tools

Pages are public to read. An access key lets your scripts create and update them through a small HTTP API.

Follow [Publishing with the API](publishing.md) to publish your first page, or open the [full API guide](/guide) for every endpoint.
